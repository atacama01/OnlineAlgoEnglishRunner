51. IMPORTANT INDEXING RULE
============================================================

This is a very important design decision.

The pseudocode language uses ONE-BASED indexing.

Arrays:

numbers[1] ... numbers[n]

Matrices:

matrix[1][1] ... matrix[rows][columns]

FOR loops normally start from 1 when counting.

Example:

For i <- 1 To 10 Do

This is the preferred educational convention.

Internally, Dart may use zero-based arrays, but convert indexes correctly at the interpreter boundary.

Never expose Dart's zero-based indexing to the pseudocode user.
